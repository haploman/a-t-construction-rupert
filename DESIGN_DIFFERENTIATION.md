# Design Differentiation Plan — A & T Construction (Rupert, ID)

Goal: create a conversion-focused contractor site that feels *clean, premium, and light* (concrete/stone), distinct from recent darker “excavation” stubs.

## Visual System
- Palette: stone/off-white base, deep slate type, dusty blue accents, safety-orange used sparingly for emphasis.
- Texture: subtle “concrete” feel via soft gradients and translucent surfaces (no heavy grunge).
- Typography: bold geometric display scale for the hero; compact uppercase nav/actions for a “trade + premium” blend.

## Signature Layout Decisions
- Sticky glass header with AT monogram mark, minimal nav, and two CTAs (Quote + Call).
- Split hero with strong negative space on the left and a photo-real composite on the right.
- Trust chips as small elevated cards (not badges/pills) to add credibility without visual noise.

## Content & Sections
- Service cards: 3×2 grid with circular line-icon treatment (distinct from icon tiles used in other stubs).
- “Typical jobs” band: pill row + CTA inside a translucent band to create rhythm between sections.
- Work section: 3 image cards with small tags (Concrete/Excavation/Prep) for quick scanning.
- Combined Service Area + Quote form section: left “map” panel + right form panel for a strong bottom-of-page conversion anchor.

## Interaction / UX
- Clear single-path conversion: Quote CTA anchors to the contact form; form posts to same-origin `/api/inquiry`.
- Chatbot: enabled by default with same-origin `/api/chatbot` to keep the widget fast and secure.
